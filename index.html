01 · Benidorm at a glance

This page is built for the questions that matter after the first property photo: where children study, how you spend a normal Tuesday, how quickly you reach the sea, healthcare, sport and the broader quality of life around the address.

A coastal city with enough infrastructure to live here, not simply visit.

Benidorm is unusually urban for the Costa Blanca. Its vertical form concentrates apartments, services, beaches and entertainment into a compact area, while the surrounding hills open quickly into golf, private residential zones and protected landscape.

For an international buyer, the larger advantage is the radius around the city: British and international schools, private healthcare, Michelin-level dining, yachting, wellness clinics and Alicante airport all sit within the practical northern Costa Blanca network.

05 · FAQ

Before choosing a property

Poniente is the clearest choice for contemporary beachfront apartments and high-rise views. Finestrat adds lower-density villas, gated developments and golf. Buyers prioritising nature, wellness and access toward Altea often look to the northern edge around Serra Gelada and the wider Altea corridor.
The city itself has year-round retail, healthcare and schools, while the wider catchment adds British and international education. For a family purchase, the exact school commute, residential micro-location, parking and off-season environment should be checked before choosing a property.
Benidorm is strongly apartment-led, including modern towers with sea views and amenities. The surrounding Finestrat area broadens the choice with detached houses and villas, often in newer residential complexes.
Building condition, community fees, legal status, parking, views that can be protected, tourist-rental rules where relevant, the quality of the immediate street, school and airport logistics, and the exit strategy all matter. These factors can be more important than a small difference in price per square metre.
