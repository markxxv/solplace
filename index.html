Benidorm

A Mediterranean city built for everyday life

Benidorm is one of the Costa Blanca’s few coastal cities where a home by the sea can work as a genuine year-round base. Poniente feels more residential, while Finestrat adds villas, privacy and elevation above the coast.

For international families, the wider area matters as much as the address itself: British schools, private healthcare, golf, marinas, wellness and Alicante airport all sit within the practical daily radius.
